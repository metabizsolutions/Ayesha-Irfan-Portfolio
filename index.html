Artist Statement

Hi! I'm Ayesha Irfan, a passionate and creative Graphics Designer, Digital Artist, and UI/UX Designer currently pursuing my Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design from Bahauddin Zakariya University, Multan. With a CGPA of 3.36/4.0 and a strong foundation in visual communication, I specialize in transforming ideas into compelling visuals.

Over the past few years, I've worked as a Freelance Digital Artist and Post Designer on Fiverr, where I've successfully delivered over 100 custom digital artworks and social media post designs for clients worldwide. I also serve as a Graphics Designer at Metabiz Solutions, where I collaborate on branding, UI/UX layouts, and promotional content that strengthens brand identity and user engagement.

From banners and illustrations to intuitive user interfaces, my goal is always to deliver designs that are not only beautiful but also functional and impactful. Let's create something amazing together!
